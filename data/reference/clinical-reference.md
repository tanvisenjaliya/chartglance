# Clinical Reference Guide: Primary Health Care Decision Thresholds

> **DRAFT – PENDING DOCTOR REVIEW**  
> *Prepared for PHC Medical Officers (MBBS) & Clinical Decision Support Systems in Indian Rural Primary Healthcare.*  
> *Source standards: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW), Government of India.*

---

## 1. NP-NCD: Diabetes Mellitus
**Programme:** *National Programme for Prevention & Control of Non-Communicable Diseases (NP-NCD)*

| Parameter | Normal Target | Yellow Alert (Sub-optimal Control) | Red Alert (Critical / Urgent Action) | Clinical Action at PHC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HbA1c** | < 7.0% | **7.0% – 8.9%** | **≥ 9.0%** (or rise ≥ 0.5% in ≥ 3 mo) | Red: Intensify therapy (add second-line oral agent or start Insulin), evaluate compliance, rule out secondary organ damage. |
| **Fasting Blood Glucose (FBG)** | 70 – 99 mg/dL | **126 – 199 mg/dL** | **≥ 200 mg/dL** | Red: Urine ketone test, assess for diabetic ketoacidosis/HHS, initiate immediate glycaemic rescue. |
| **Post-Prandial Glucose (PPBG)** | < 140 mg/dL | **140 – 199 mg/dL** | **≥ 200 mg/dL** | Yellow: Diet/lifestyle modification, titration of Metformin/Sulfonylurea. |

## 2. NP-NCD: Hypertension
**Programme:** *National Programme for Prevention & Control of Non-Communicable Diseases (NP-NCD)*

| Blood Pressure Stage | Systolic (mmHg) | Diastolic (mmHg) | Alert Level | Clinical Action at PHC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Normal** | < 120 | and < 80 | Green | Annual screening. |
| **Elevated / Pre-HTN** | 120 – 139 | or 80 – 89 | Routine | Lifestyle intervention (dietary salt restriction < 5 g/day, brisk walking). |
| **Stage 1 Hypertension** | 140 – 159 | or 90 – 99 | Routine | Initiate single anti-hypertensive (e.g., Amlodipine 5 mg OD or Telmisartan 40 mg OD). |
| **Stage 2 Hypertension** | 160 – 179 | or 100 – 109 | **Yellow Alert** | Dual drug combination therapy, review within 14 days. |
| **Hypertensive Urgency / Crisis** | **≥ 180** | **or ≥ 110** | **Red Alert** | Immediate rest, oral anti-hypertensive, screen for acute target organ damage (chest pain, dyspnoea, neurological deficits). Urgent referral if symptomatic. |

## 3. NTEP: Tuberculosis Treatment & Adherence
**Programme:** *National Tuberculosis Elimination Programme (NTEP)*

| Parameter / Milestone | Target Schedule | Yellow Alert (Treatment Warning) | Red Alert (High Risk Interruption) | Clinical Action at PHC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Daily FDC Pick-up** | Every 28 days (monthly blister pack) | **1 – 13 days overdue** from scheduled date | **> 14 days overdue** without medicine supply | Red: Immediate active home-tracing via ASHA / Senior Treatment Supervisor (STS), Nikshay alert, sputum re-evaluation for loss-to-follow-up / drug resistance. |
| **Sputum Smear / NAAT** | Baseline, end of IP (2 mo), end of CP | Delayed sputum test by > 1 wk | Positive smear at end of Intensive Phase | Red: Extend IP by 1 month if indicated, order rapid drug susceptibility test (CBNAAT/TrueNat) for Rifampicin resistance. |

## 4. Anaemia Mukt Bharat (AMB) & Maternal ANC Guidelines
**Programme:** *Anaemia Mukt Bharat (AMB) & Reproductive, Maternal, Newborn, Child and Adolescent Health (RMNCH+A)*

| Target Group | Non-Anaemic | Mild Anaemia | Moderate (Yellow Alert) | Severe Anaemia (Red Alert) | Very Severe |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pregnant Women** | ≥ 11.0 g/dL | 10.0 – 10.9 g/dL | **7.0 – 9.9 g/dL** | **< 7.0 g/dL** | < 4.0 g/dL |
| **Adolescent Girls (10–19y)** | ≥ 12.0 g/dL | 11.0 – 11.9 g/dL | **8.0 – 10.9 g/dL** | **< 8.0 g/dL** | < 5.0 g/dL |
| **Adolescent Boys (10–14y)** | ≥ 12.0 g/dL | 11.0 – 11.9 g/dL | **8.0 – 10.9 g/dL** | **< 8.0 g/dL** | < 5.0 g/dL |
| **Adolescent Boys (15y+)** | ≥ 13.0 g/dL | 12.0 – 12.9 g/dL | **8.0 – 11.9 g/dL** | **< 8.0 g/dL** | < 5.0 g/dL |

*Clinical Protocols for Pregnancy:*
- **Mild/Moderate (7.0–10.9 g/dL):** Two IFA tablets daily (each containing 60 mg elemental iron + 500 mcg folic acid).
- **Severe (< 7.0 g/dL):** **Red Flag.** Immediate referral to CHC/Sub-District Hospital for IV Ferric Carboxymaltose (FCM) or blood transfusion prep. Never rely on oral iron alone.

## 5. Universal Immunisation Programme (UIP)
**Programme:** *Universal Immunisation Programme (UIP) / National Immunisation Schedule (NIS)*

| Age / Period | Vaccines Administered | Overdue Window (Yellow Alert) | Critical Default (Red Alert) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **At Birth** | BCG, OPV-0, Hepatitis B birth dose | > 24 hours for Hep-B; > 2 weeks for BCG | > 1 year for BCG |
| **6, 10, 14 Weeks** | Pentavalent (1,2,3), OPV (1,2,3), Rotavirus (1,2,3), fIPV (6, 14 wks), PCV (6, 14 wks) | > 2 weeks past due date | Missed Pentavalent-1 by > 8 weeks (drop-out risk) |
| **9 – 12 Months** | MR 1st dose, JE-1 (endemic districts), PCV booster | > 1 month past 9th month | Unvaccinated against Measles past 12 months |
| **16 – 24 Months** | MR 2nd dose, DPT booster-1, OPV booster, JE-2 | > 2 months past 16th month | Missed MR-2 |
| **5 – 6 Years** | DPT booster-2 | > 6 months past 5th year | Missed second booster |
| **10 & 16 Years** | Td (Tetanus & adult Diphtheria) | > 6 months past due age | Unvaccinated for tetanus |
| **Pregnant Women** | Td-1 early in pregnancy; Td-2 4 weeks later (or Td booster if received in prior 3 yrs) | > 4 weeks gap between doses | Unimmunised at delivery |

## 6. Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) & Renal Function
**Programme:** *NP-NCD & Indian Society of Nephrology (ISN) Clinical Practice Guidelines*

| Stage / Parameter | Serum Creatinine | Estimated GFR (eGFR) | Alert Level | Clinical Action at PHC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Stage 1 & 2 (Early)** | Normal (0.6 – 1.2 mg/dL) | ≥ 60 mL/min/1.73m² + proteinuria | Routine | Tight BP (< 130/80) & glucose control; start ACEi/ARB if albuminuria present. |
| **Stage 3 (Moderate)** | **1.4 – 1.9 mg/dL** | **30 – 59 mL/min/1.73m²** | **Yellow Alert** | Reduce Metformin dose (max 1000 mg/day if eGFR 30–44); stop NSAIDs; nephrologist tele-consultation. |
| **Stage 4 & 5 (Severe)** | **≥ 2.0 mg/dL** | **< 30 mL/min/1.73m²** | **Red Alert** | **Contraindicated: Metformin, SGLT2i, NSAIDs.** High risk of lactic acidosis & fluid overload. Urgent tertiary nephrology referral. |
